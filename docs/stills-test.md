# Do sharper stills help the locate check?

Generated by `backend/eval/bench_stills.py`: Muse Spark 1.3 on 24 moments of one expert run, 2 passes, same crop for both qualities. Full-quality recording frames stand in for real stills, which have not been tested yet. An expert run is cleaner than a novice's, and labels are good to about 5 s.

| Image | Exactly right | Within one step | Mean steps off | Unclear | Exact at confidence >= 0.85 | Same on repeat | Latency median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full quality at 1080 wide | 0.75 | 1.0 | 0.25 | 0 | 0.78 (n=45) | 0.92 | 1.51 s |
| Simulated stream | 0.62 | 0.92 | 0.65 | 0 | 0.73 (n=30) | 0.83 | 1.59 s |

The first step's moments are easy (a notebook or a torn sheet) and make up 10 of the 24. Without them:

| Image | Exactly right | Within one step | Exact at confidence >= 0.85 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full quality at 1080 wide | 15/28 (54%) | 28/28 | 14/24 (58%) |
| Simulated stream | 10/28 (36%) | 24/28 | 2/10 (20%) |

Every wrong full-quality answer was one step too far ahead, or a mid-step shape read as finished. Sharper images remove the wild
answers but not this bias. The simulated stream's 36% matches the 37% measured on the real novice stream (docs/open-weights.md).
